Xorvio brings your live channels, films, series, media servers and your own video folders into one calm, fast app, with a full guide and recording built in.

The tour

One window. Your whole library.

Live TV, films, series, recordings and your watchlist share one window, one search and one player. Pick a screen to look around.

Home

It knows what's next.

Pick up exactly where you stopped. Continue Watching keeps your place in every film and lines up the next episode of every series, with your watchlist, your recordings and tonight's best on TV right below.

  • Up Next, automatically. Finish an episode and the next one is waiting, with new episodes flagged as they arrive.
  • Charts and lists from Trakt. What's trending, the week's most watched, any public list and your own, shown as the copies you can actually play.
  • A row for every library. Each media folder and media server gets its own row, and every row can be shown, hidden or moved.

Films & series

Pages worth lingering on.

Every title gets a full-window page: artwork edge to edge, the story, the people who made it, and exactly what you're about to play.

Measured, not guessed

4K, HDR and surround badges come from the stream itself, so you know what you'll get before you press Play.

One poster, every copy

A film several sources carry is one title. Its page lists every version, from your provider, your server or your folders.

Richer with TMDB

Title logos, wordless backdrops, cast photos, episode stills and More Like This, when you'd like them.

Your languages, remembered

Choose audio and subtitles once. Xorvio remembers them for the series and applies them to the next episode.

Live TV

A guide that feels like home.

A preview that keeps playing while you browse, the program in detail beside it, and a guide that reaches eight days back and as far ahead as your listings go.

  • Pause and rewind live TV. Keep up to two hours of a channel on your computer, and step back to what you missed.
  • Catch-up and replay. Play what already aired wherever your provider keeps an archive.
  • Zap like a remote. ↑ and ↓ change channel, numbers tune one, L jumps back to the last.

Recording

Your own DVR, built in.

Record what's on now, or anything coming up from the guide, your watchlist or a search. Recordings play in the same player and keep your place.

  • Series recordings. New episodes only, on one channel or any, keeping all of them or just the latest few.
  • Save from catch-up. Missed it? Save a program your provider still has in its archive.
  • Skip the ad breaks. Breaks are found after recording, marked on the timeline and skipped with one press, or automatically.

The player

Plays right where you are.

Video opens inside the window, not in another one. Shrink it to a mini player while you browse, pop it out to float above your work, send it to the TV, or go full screen.

  • Mini player
  • Pop-out window
  • Picture in Picture
  • AirPlay
  • Chromecast and Google TV
  • Previews while scrubbing
  • Audio and subtitle tracks
  • Blu-ray and DVD subtitles
  • Open in IINA, VLC or Infuse
  • Automatic reconnect

Multiview

Four at once.

Put up to four channels, films or episodes side by side, or keep one small in a corner and swap it in with X: a film through the ad breaks, with the game waiting in the corner. Sound follows whichever one you click or the number you press, and Xorvio tells you before you go past the connections your provider allows. Pause any of them, or all at once, then carry on from where you left them or go back to live.

Watchlist

One list for everything.

Save films, series and upcoming live programs to the same list. Xorvio reminds you before a program starts, and one click takes you to the channel. Connect Trakt and it stays in step everywhere you watch.

Your sources

Bring everything you have.

Your provider's channels, your media server, the folders on your drives: Xorvio puts them in one library, with one search and one watchlist across all of them.

  • IPTVPlaylists and providersM3U and M3U8 playlists from an address or a file, and Xtream Codes logins with your provider's details, artwork and replay.
  • EPGXMLTV guidesPlain or compressed, added to any source, matched to channels by ID or by name.
  • SERVERPlex, Jellyfin and EmbySign in to Plex or connect a server by address. Libraries play straight from the server, and what you've watched goes both ways.
  • FOLDERYour own videosFolders on your computer, a drive or a network share, with artwork and details read from beside your files, and a library page to tidy them up.
  • ADD-ONAdd-onsCatalog and stream add-ons that speak the open Stremio add-on protocol. Xorvio ships with none and recommends none: paste the link of one you trust.
  • DEMODemo libraryClearly labeled sample channels, films and series, built in, to look around before you add anything.

Xorvio doesn't include, sell or recommend any channels, content or add-ons. Use only sources you have the right to use.

And so much more

Thought through, down to the details.

Keep watching while you browse

Shrink what's playing to a mini player in any corner, and keep exploring. Click it to bring the picture back.

Plays the hard ones

Xorvio carries its own FFmpeg, so MKV, HEVC, AV1, DTS and Dolby audio play without installing anything else.

Backup and sync

Back up your library, with logins sealed by a passphrase if you like, and keep computers in step through a folder you already share. No server.

Library profiles

Cut a huge catalog down to the languages and genres each person actually watches, each with its own Home.

Parental controls

Lock categories behind a PIN everywhere they appear: browsing, Home, search and channel changes.

Keyboard first

Browse, zap, seek in 10 or 60 seconds, open Multiview and start Picture in Picture, all from the keyboard.

Tidy names

Prefixes, tags and clutter are cleaned from channel and title names for display. Your source is never changed.

Dark, light, or both

A carefully tuned dark palette for movie nights and a light one for daytime, following your computer or set by hand.

Your library stays yours.

No account. No ads. No analytics. Your sources, history and watchlist live on your computer, and your logins are kept in your system's own keychain.
